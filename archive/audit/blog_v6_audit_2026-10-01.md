# Sixth audit: v6 blog drafts (read-only; the only file written is the plan file)

## Summary

**Numbers.** Almost every number traces to a committed file, with the correct scope, sign, rounding and sample. I checked them against:
- YB-0035: `results.json`, `exploratory_audit2/3.json`, `seed7_counts.json`
- YB-0042: `results.json`, README
- YB-0031 and YB-0015 `results.json`
- the artifacts: `model_sizes`, `judge_timing`, `shift_verification`, `c1_github_server_timestamps`, and the alignment log in `yb0035_evidence`

I also recomputed the seed-7 and seed-8 counts from the raw episodes. Both come out at exactly 600 recorded, 535 answered, 40 wrong and 65 never answered. The question sets differ, so this is a coincidence, not a data mix-up. The analysis code did not change between the pre-registration (bb36912) and the results commit (ea2f858).

**Blocking issue.** The live public repository that both posts send readers to does not yet contain the second test (YB-0042).

**Main substantive problems:**
- The "internal-state monitoring as a class" conclusion goes beyond what was tested.
- The seed-8 comparison against the length baseline is degenerate (length caught 0).
- No audit has yet covered YB-0042 or the NVIDIA section.
- The technical draft leaves out the pre-registered race the regulator lost (YB-0031).
- The descriptions of the audits are stale or inconsistent.

**Verdicts:**
- **Plain draft:** not publishable until C1 is fixed. After that, publishable with the fixes listed below.
- **Technical draft:** not publishable until C1 is fixed. After that, publishable with the fixes listed below.

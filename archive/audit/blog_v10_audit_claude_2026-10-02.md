# Audit of the v10 blog drafts (2 October 2026)

**Verdicts**
- **Technical draft:** not publishable as written. It can be published once C1–C3 and M1–M8 are fixed; the remaining items are minor.
- **Plain-English draft:** not publishable as written, for the same reasons, plus M5 ("can't jailbreak it") and M6 ("timestamped in public before each test"). After those, it is publishable with the minor fixes.

## Summary

Every number for YB-0035, YB-0042, YB-0045 and the earlier studies (YB-0031, YB-0015) traces to a committed file. The only number problems are two labelling slips (m2, m3).

Every YB-0044 number also matches exactly when I recompute it from the per-episode files:
- **Arms:** 19 wrong commits stopped and 3 correct blocked in arm B; 93 stopped and 16 held in arm C.
- **Hypotheses:** H1 +0.536 [+0.456, +0.616]; H2 79.6% [72.0%, 87.1%].
- **Timings:** races won and lost at 8.4 / 2.9 / 8.2 s; 14 + 2 correct holds; release delay 7.4 s median, 9.0 s 90th percentile.
- **Policy latency:** 5.47 s and 3.55 s.

The problems are where the evidence lives and how the results are framed, not the arithmetic:
- The YB-0044 results are not committed and not in the public snapshot.
- The setup document the drafts cite does not contain the tunnel-failure story.
- Arm C's 93 of 93 is guaranteed by its design.
- Live enforcement latency varied far more than the drafts say.
- No audit has covered YB-0044.
